Heyyyyyyyy thanks for visiting me:D

I completed my PhD at Imperial College London in May 2026, supervised by [Prof. Danilo P. Mandić](https://www.commsp.ee.ic.ac.uk/~mandic/index.htm). My research focuses on [Generative Language Model Compression with Algebraic Approaches](https://hdl.handle.net/10044/1/128992), including tensor decomposition, structural pruning, and deployment on resource-constrained devices (e.g. Raspberry Pi).

I am also the founder of [Facthink](https://facthink.com/), building [Graph-Hypoth](https://github.com/Mingxue-Xu/graph-hypoth), a multi-agent system that develops research claims into candidate hypotheses and literature-grounded experiment plans.

<u>I am currently on the job market, looking at the intersection of research and engineering.</u> Please do [keep in touch](mailto:mmercyxu@outlook.com) for any potential opportunities!

Previously, I obtained my BEng at China University of Petroleum - Beijing, and did my MEng at University of Science and Technology of China, under the supervision of [Prof. Xiang-Yang Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JURtNb0AAAAJ&hl=en). Both my BEng and MEng are in Computer Science and Technology. I interned at [Baidu PaddlePaddle](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle) as a technical product manager, serving for the developer community of [PaddleSpeech](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleSpeech).
